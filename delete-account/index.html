Delete Your Account & Data

Last updated: May 29, 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your Winkidoo account and the personal data associated with it, in accordance with Google Play's User Data policy.

App: Winkidoo (com.winkidoo.winkidoo)
Developer: Winkidoo

How to Request Deletion

You can request deletion of your account and associated data in either of the following ways:

Option 1 — In the App

  1. Open Winkidoo and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

Option 2 — By Email

Send an email to privacy@winkidoo.com from the email address associated with your account, with the subject line "Delete My Account". We will verify your identity and process the request.

Email a Deletion Request

We process verified deletion requests within 30 days. You will receive a confirmation email once your account and data have been deleted.

What Data Is Deleted

When your account is deleted, we permanently remove the following:

DataDeleted?
Account details (email, display name, profile photo)Yes — deleted
Surprise content you created (text, photos, voice notes)Yes — deleted
Battle chat messages and persuasion historyYes — deleted
Custom AI judges and uploaded judge imagesYes — deleted
Character chat rooms, messages, and friend connectionsYes — deleted
XP, streaks, quests, achievements, and game progressYes — deleted
Push notification tokensYes — deleted

What May Be Retained, and For How Long

A limited set of data may be retained only where required:

  • Purchase / subscription records — retained by our payment processor (RevenueCat) and the app store (Google Play) as required for financial, tax, and legal recordkeeping. We do not control these statutory retention periods.
  • Anonymised, aggregated analytics — data that can no longer be linked to you may be retained for product improvement.
  • Security and abuse logs — retained for up to 90 days for fraud prevention and security, with personal identifiers masked.
Shared couple content: Winkidoo is a two-person game. Content you explicitly shared with your partner (e.g. a revealed surprise in their vault) may remain visible to them after your account is deleted, as it also belongs to their account. Contact us if you need such content removed.

Questions

For any questions about account or data deletion, contact us at privacy@winkidoo.com. See also our Privacy Policy.
